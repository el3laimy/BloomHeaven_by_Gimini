# 📦 BloomHaven — Post-CVP Feature Backlog
## Shelved Features Reserved for Commercial Scaling & Full Release

The following features were consciously frozen during **Phase 0 (Reality Check)** to guarantee shipping velocity, visual consistency, and a razor-sharp 30–45 minute Core Commercial Slice.

---

### 1. Apiary & Honey Harvesting System (Bees)
- **Concept:** Wooden beehives with worker bees producing honeycomb and bottled royal jelly.
- **Trigger to Unfreeze:** Once the core floral economy and customer ordering loop are validated by 50+ external playtesters.

### 2. Perfume Distillery Laboratory
- **Concept:** Converting harvested petals into high-tier bottled fragrances and aromatics (`data/perfumes.json`).
- **Trigger to Unfreeze:** Phase 4 economy validation with positive player retention beyond 45 minutes.

### 3. Regional Flower Festivals & Contests
- **Concept:** Timed weekly floral arrangement competitions (Garden Cups, Golden Ribbon judged by visiting botanical inspectors).
- **Trigger to Unfreeze:** Full Game Release milestone.

### 4. Advanced Mendelian Allele Segregation
- **Concept:** 4-locus procedural genetics calculating incomplete dominance, rare mutations, and vigor modifiers (`scripts/genetics/`).
- **Trigger to Unfreeze:** Post-CVP expansion DLC / Breeding Lab update. CVP strictly uses authored **Curated Breeding**.

### 5. Dynamic Weather & 4 Seasons
- **Concept:** Autumn foliage changes, winter frost requiring glass cloches, spring pollination surges, and summer heatwaves.
- **Trigger to Unfreeze:** Kickstarter / Publisher expansion scope.

### 6. Extended Flora Catalog (100+ Flowers)
- **Concept:** Sunflowers, Orchids, Carnations, Lilies, Hydrangeas, Dahlias, Peonies, and exotic tropical breeds.
- **Trigger to Unfreeze:** Post-CVP content roadmap once art pipeline templates are firmly established.
